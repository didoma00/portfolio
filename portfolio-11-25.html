Brasa

Brasa

25 years of satellite fire detections (2000–2025) on one consistent scale. Brasa harmonizes MODIS and VIIRS data from NASA FIRMS so any two years can be compared directly — explored through an interactive 3D globe, a month-by-month calendar, "fire rings" and downloadable area records. Built for NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026.

3D globe · NASA FIRMS · MODIS / VIIRS · Data viz · Cloud Run
